# Astronomy planning app research — 2026-09-26

Research for the Dark Sky Shot Planner iOS field companion. Product features below are claims in official product pages, App Store or Google Play listings, and vendor documentation, not results of hands-on tests. An unlisted feature is **unknown**, not absent. Recent-change window: 2026-08-28 through 2026-09-26; older documentation supplies product background. Prices and entitlements can change.

## Product recommendation

The combination “forecast + light pollution + Milky Way + AR” is already marketed by several products. A more defensible goal is **one explicit, site-and-shot-specific usable window**: chosen target direction and altitude, local horizon, terrain-blocked Moon, moon-free astronomical darkness, cloud spread over the *same* window, and a traceable reason for the verdict. This is a proposed product position based on published descriptions, not a verified claim that no competitor computes it.

The current [iOS plan](PLAN.md) has most components, but its headline “minutes of moon-free astronomical darkness” is already a PhotoPills concept. The screen should say what those minutes mean for the chosen shot, and when the target clears the site horizon. Keep the worst-factor verdict and expose the missing/stale inputs. Add a visible comparison of two or three saved spots for the same target and night only after Tonight/Week/Map are coherent. Validate by timing a real decision against opening PhotoPills + a forecast app, not by counting features.

Keep access status separate from land-management boundaries: a public-land polygon cannot establish nighttime entry, road condition, or camping permission. Map products also distinguish VIIRS upward radiance from modeled zenith brightness and measured SQM. Label the layer, year/resolution and source; do not turn a radiance value into a precise site darkness promise. [Light Pollution Map explains these limits](https://www.lightpollutionmap.info/help.html). [meteoblue explains](https://content.meteoblue.com/en/private-customers/website-help/outdoor-and-sports/astronomy-seeing) why clouds and seeing are separate variables.

For web and native continuity, define a versioned, shareable plan URL carrying place, date/time, target and composition state, with a documented privacy choice for precise saved coordinates. [TPE](https://photoephemeris.com/en/web/) already shares plans to its app; [PlanIt Earth](https://www.planitphoto.com/blog/planit-earth-web-app) now provides substantial browser planning. A Home Screen web app can receive user-visible [Web Push on iOS](https://webkit.org/blog/13878/web-push-for-web-apps-on-ios-and-ipados/), but timely weather-change alerts still require server-side evaluation and push; this does not fit the current stateless Worker without a new design decision. Local background checks on iOS are best-effort.

## iOS 27 / iPhone 18 Pro Max

[iOS 27 was released September 14](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/major-updates-for-apples-software-platforms-are-now-available/); [iPhone 18 Pro Max became available September 18](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/apple-debuts-iphone-18-pro-and-iphone-18-pro-max/). The phone has a 48 MP variable-aperture main camera (ƒ/1.48, ƒ/1.8, ƒ/2.8, ƒ/4.0), LiDAR, dual-frequency GPS and a 1-nit display minimum in [Apple’s specifications](https://www.apple.com/ie/iphone-18-pro/specs/). These are hardware opportunities, not reasons to make core planning depend on the latest phone.

## Immediate handoff to the coordinating agent

1. **Do not add another feature track yet.** Finish integration and make a single site-to-shot plan truthful on device. The competitive advantage is the joined decision and evidence, not feature count.
2. **Make forecast validity explicit in the UI and contract.** Preserve model name/run time, valid hour, geographic coverage, interpolation, freshness and missing status. Do not average model disagreement away or treat unavailable as clear. [Windy’s model-variable coverage table](https://api4.windy.com/point-forecast/docs) demonstrates why each model/variable needs its own availability check.
3. **Review two wording conflicts in the current plan.** M8 BGAppRefreshTask cannot promise prompt weather alerts. The iOS routing rule that red mode has “no white or blue pixel anywhere, including alerts” cannot be guaranteed for system-owned alerts, keyboards or permission surfaces. Ask Brian to scope that guarantee to app-owned surfaces, document unavoidable system UI, and check the result on device.
4. **Keep the owner’s personal-first licensing boundary visible.** The plan itself marks 7Timer non-commercial and blocks public App Store distribution without permission; re-audit every external tile/feed and attribution before publishing. [Clear Sky Chart restricts republication](https://www.cleardarksky.com/csk/admin.html); [Light Pollution Map requests contact for service reuse](https://www.lightpollutionmap.info/help.html). Do not copy competitor tiles by URL.
5. **Validate the unusual value in the field.** At two or three saved sites, compare predicted Moon/target clearance with visible horizon, log forecast versus actual sky, test offline cached ages and the physical phone’s red/AR/power behavior. The current DEM validation in PLAN.md is a single site at 0.494° RMS versus a 0.5° gate, so more terrain types matter before claiming broad accuracy.
6. **Before public release, check positioning and name.** [DarkScout](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/astro-planner-darkscout/id6760223860) and [Dark Skies](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dark-skies-astrophotography/id843351673) create likely search confusion for “Dark Sky Shot Planner.” This is a discoverability observation, not trademark advice.
